# Product

Kadoku is a conversation-first gift shopping app. The goal is to help people choose a thoughtful gift without browsing a large catalog.

## Experience

- Start with text or voice: recipient, occasion, interests, and budget.
- Show one top recommendation from the store catalog, with up to two alternatives.
- Refine the result in the same conversation; keep budget and exclusions.
- Add a gift note, check out, and save people and birthdays for future occasions.

## Principles

- Never invent product availability, prices, or stock.
- Keep recommendations focused and mobile-first.
- Require explicit buyer approval before capturing PayPal payments.
- Keep sample data optional; build toward real supplier-backed commerce.

## Next

Verified inventory, shipping and fulfillment, Sandbox refunds/reconciliation, then PayPal Live after production security and operational checks.
